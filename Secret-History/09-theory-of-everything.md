## Problems with Scientific Theories

### Problems with the Big Bang

- Universe should expand **linearly** if starting from a single explosion.
- Reality: Expands **irregularly**:
  - Different parts move at different speeds.
  - Some galaxies faster than others.
- Explanation: **Dark energy** (80–90% of universe).
  - "What is dark energy?" → We don't know.
  - Criticized as "cheating" (like adding "dark energy" to fix a wrong math answer: 19887 + 25 = 20 + dark energy).
- Alternative: **Multiple Big Bangs** → better explains irregularities.
- Issue: Current model is a rigid paradigm; scientists resist change despite flaws.

### Problems with Evolution (Works for Animals, Not Humans)

1. **Gradual progress** expected, but:
   - Humans share **99.9% DNA** with apes.
   - Huge differences unexplained.
2. Humans ~**200,000 years old**; only ~**50,000 years** of recorded history → Where are the missing 150,000 years?
3. Should be **many human types** (e.g., six fingers, three eyes).
   - Only **one Homo sapiens**.
   - Other species (Neanderthals, etc.) supposedly outcompeted—but doesn't fit diversity expectation.

### Problems with the Human Mind (Neuroscience)

  - Too much info → brain would "explode."
  - Filters important vs. unimportant → long-term memory.

- Personalities seem **innate** (speaker has 3 kids—all distinct from birth).
  - Not from parents' DNA (eye color, height yes; personality no).
  - You are not a composite of your parents' personalities.
